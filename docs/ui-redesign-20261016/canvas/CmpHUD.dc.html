What changes

  • Now about 14 things float on the screen in 5 different styles (see-through mute and Run, a white chat bubble, a pink Play, a purple Jump). The mockup groups them into 3 zones, all in one style.
  • The see-through grey buttons (sound, Run) are hard to see on the bright world. Every button becomes solid with an outline.
  • The goal chip says "Talk to Grandpa Owl" but shows no progress. The goal card shows the steps as dots and has a hear-again button.
  • The message "A surprise gift is near…" covers the middle, where the child is. Messages move to the top, or into a bubble beside the thing.
  • Coins and stars show all the time, so rewards can fly into them.
  • Your choice: the mockup has no mini-map, clock, mic, Run or camera. Keep the mini-map top-right and put the rest in a "more" tray?

Rules for this screen

HUD-1Three zones only: top = who I am and what I have; under it = today's goal; bottom = moving and menus.
HUD-2At most 8 buttons always showing on a phone. Anything else goes in a "more" tray.
HUD-3The middle of the screen (where the child stands) stays clear. Messages go at the top or beside the thing they talk about, and leave after 4 seconds.
HUD-4The goal card always shows progress and a hear-again button. Tapping it opens the adventure.
HUD-5Moving stick bottom-left, Jump (biggest, 96 px) and Talk bottom-right, menus in one bar along the bottom.
HUD-6Something you can pick up glows gold with a small bubble ("An egg is here!").
