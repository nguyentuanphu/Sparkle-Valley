What changes

  • Now it's one long list: Family missions, Side stories, Treasure chests, Main adventure. A child can't tell which one to do. The mockup shows one adventure for today.
  • Now the tasks are sentences ("Do it for real, then a grown-up holds ✓"). The mockup uses 3 picture steps of a few words each, and Grandpa Owl says them.
  • Now the reward isn't shown. The mockup shows the chest before they start.
  • The big pale tick circles look like buttons but mean "done". Progress uses one style instead: tick, gold glow, dashed.
  • The other lists (family missions, side stories, chests) stay, under a "More adventures" button, mainly for big kids and grown-ups.

Rules for this screen

ADV-1One adventure on the screen at a time. Other lists sit behind one "More adventures" button.
ADV-2Always 3 steps. Each has a picture or number and at most 6 words.
ADV-3A character introduces it in a speech bubble (2 lines at most, recorded voice, no child's name in the spoken line).
ADV-4The reward is shown before the child starts.
ADV-5Week stamps only fill up. A missed day never takes one away, and there are no timers for under-5s.
ADV-6One green "Let's go!" button at the bottom. It closes the window and points the way.
