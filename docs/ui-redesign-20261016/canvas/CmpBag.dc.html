What changes

  • Before the first item, the screen stacks a pets bar, 3 counters, category chips, a "Food" heading with "Cook at a cooking pot…", and another heading. The items start halfway down.
  • Item names are small (about 12 px) and include prices ("Red ruby · $25"), which a 4-year-old can't read.
  • Sections fold open and closed with small ▼ arrows, which is a grown-up pattern. The mockup puts the items straight on screen in a 3 × 3 grid of big tiles.
  • Tapping a tile selects it and shows one card with what it's for and two big buttons ("Give", "Eat it?"). This fits the r79 "Bag things explain themselves" work.
  • Coins, orbs and leafies already sit in the top counters (game screen), so they don't need repeating here.

Rules for this screen

BAG-1Items are the first thing under the title. 3 tiles per row on a phone, picture 60 px centred, name at most 2 lines and at least 15 px, count badge bottom-right.
BAG-2Tapping a tile only selects it (gold ring). Nothing is eaten, given or sold by one tap.
BAG-3The action card shows the picture, name, one line on what it's for, and at most 2 buttons (green main and paper second).
BAG-4Kinds of things (food, treasures, toys, gifts) are tabs, never folding sections.
BAG-5No prices on children's tiles. Prices show only in the Shop and in the action card for big kids.
BAG-6Gift boxes look like wrapped gifts, and their main button is always "Give it".
