What changes

  • A new player sees a wall of 36 identical purple "?" tiles ("My treasures 0/36"). There's nothing to look forward to and nothing to be proud of.
  • The mockup splits it into pages of 9 by theme (Animals, Food, Places, Festivals), each tab with its own colour.
  • Each missing sticker shows a hint of where to find it ("Lives by the pond"), so the book tells the child what to do next.
  • A progress bar and a prize for a full page (golden frame, a new song) give a reason to finish.
  • The scrapbook (decorate with photos) and treasures stay. Treasures become one of the pages.

Rules for this screen

STK-1Pages of exactly 9 (3 × 3), one theme each. The tab colour is the theme colour.
STK-2Stickers they have: full colour plus name. Missing ones: grey shape, "?" and a hint of where to find it (spoken on tap).
STK-3Never an empty page. The first page always has 2–3 stickers a new player gets in their first minutes.
STK-4Every page shows a count, a progress bar and its prize at the bottom.
STK-5A new sticker keeps a purple "New!" badge until it's tapped. The Stickers button shows how many are new.
STK-6Tapping a sticker says its name in both languages and plays its sound.
