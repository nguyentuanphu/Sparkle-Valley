What changes

  • Now Grown-ups is one narrow window over the game with every setting in one long scroll: Home Screen, privacy, captions, language, voices, children, devices… The children come only after several screens of settings.
  • The mockup is full screen with a side menu (Children, Play time, Learning, Voices and sound, Family devices, Settings), one topic per page.
  • Children come first: each child gets a card with picture, age group, what they did this week, and difficulty.
  • A new "Daily adventure and play time" group holds the new features (adventure on/off, Owl says goodnight).
  • All current settings stay. They only move into the section they belong to. "Save & close" can become saving automatically.

Rules for this screen

GU-1Full screen with a section menu: down the left on a tablet or computer, tabs along the top on a phone. Never one long scroll.
GU-2Children is always the first section.
GU-3Each setting is one row: label on the left, control on the right, at most one line of help under it.
GU-4Same kit pieces as the children's screens, but calmer: fewer colours, text 15–17 px, buttons at least 44 px.
GU-5A change saves as soon as it's made and shows a small "Saved" tick.
GU-6A new setting goes into an existing section. A new section needs the owner's yes.
