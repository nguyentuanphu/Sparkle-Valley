Rules for every screen · UI-1 to UI-10
Every screen follows these. Each screen below adds its own few rules. A change that breaks one needs the owner's yes first.
UI-1
One kit. Screens are built only from the kit pieces: Panel, Main button, Second button, Round button, Tile, Tab, Chip, Counter. A new look goes into the kit first, never into one screen.
UI-2
Colours have one job. Green = go / yes. Gold = earned or picked. Pink = close / back. Sky = hear again / info. Purple = new / magic. Never use a colour for another job.
UI-3
Four text sizes. Baloo 2 for titles, Fredoka for the rest: 40, 28, 22, 18 px. Nothing under 15 px on a children's screen; 15 px only for item names under pictures.
UI-4
Big targets. Children's buttons at least 56 px, the main one 64 px, Jump 96 px; at least 8 px between them. Grown-ups' at least 44 px.
UI-5
One main action. Each screen has at most one green Main button, at the bottom where a thumb reaches. Close is always the pink round button, top right.
UI-6
Pictures first. Every choice has a picture; words go under it, at most 2 lines. An under-5 can use every screen without reading, and tapping any label says it.
UI-7
Solid, outlined, sinking. Panels and buttons: solid colour (never see-through over the world), 3 px ink outline, 4–6 px solid shadow. Pressed = sinks into its shadow.
UI-8
Feel and sound. Windows open with a 180 ms bounce and close with a 120 ms shrink; every tap has a sound. "Reduce motion" turns the bounce off.
UI-9
Nine at a time. A phone screen shows at most 9 choices (3 × 3). More choices go on tabs or pages, not a long scrolling list.
UI-10
Progress looks the same everywhere. Done = green tick. Now = gold glow. Not yet = dashed outline. Missing = grey shape with "?".
The handbook's hard rules still hold: everything bilingual with T(), no phone emoji (drawings from js/art.js), recorded voices, and nothing removed without the owner asking. The mockups leave out some current buttons (mini-map, clock, mic, Run, camera); each screen below says where they would go, and that is the owner's choice.
