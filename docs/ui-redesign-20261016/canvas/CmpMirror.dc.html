What changes

  • The r79 mirror already does what the mockup does: big picture, tap to wear, tap again to take off, no Remove buttons. It's the closest of all the screens.
  • The picture box takes about a quarter of the screen. In the mockup the child is the hero, at about 40 %.
  • The 6 category buttons are small line icons with number badges, some showing "0". The mockup shows only categories that have clothes, as big labelled tabs.
  • The instruction line "Tap a picture to wear it. Tap it again to take it off." moves under the clothes, smaller, and is spoken once.
  • The mirror sits inside the Shop tab. It could open straight from the player card ("Dress up").

Rules for this screen

MIR-1The child's picture is the biggest thing on the screen (at least 40 % of its height).
MIR-2Only clothes the child owns, from their own set. Empty categories are hidden.
MIR-3Tap = wear (gold ring), tap again = take off. No Remove buttons (owner, r79).
MIR-4The picture changes at once, with a sparkle and a "swish" sound.
MIR-5Category tabs have a picture and a word, at least 50 px tall, at most 4 showing at once.
MIR-6A bubble above the picture names what they're wearing, so the child hears the clothes' words.
