# r90: twice the games and stories, unlocked by playing (brief)

Owner, 23 Oct 2026 AEDT, in the Release chat (pop-ups). **The full plan: [r90-plan.md](r90-plan.md).** **This is the whole of round r90**; r89 ships first with what
is ready (website, houses, drawing games, autoplay fixes, launch pictures). Nothing here is built yet: the plan with
the exact new games and stories goes to the owner (pop-up) before building.

## What the owner asked
- **Twice as many games in every category.** Today (with r89's ten drawing games): 99 games. Learning tab 32
  (numbers 7, words 5, shapes 7, me 3, money 10), Folk games 18 (Vietnamese 8, play outside 10), Animals and music 12
  (animals 7, music 5), Pictures 25 (15 + the 10 drawing games), Going places 8, Big thinkers 4. So about 100 new games.
- **Twice as many stories in every shelf, more for Australia, and a new shelf "World".** Today 38 stories: Vietnam 20,
  Australia 6, China 6, India 6. Each story has English and Vietnamese narration and its own 3D film.
- **Unlocking:** the new half of each category opens only after the child has played each game already open in that
  category 3 times. The end of each category shows a note saying so ("To unlock more, finish each game here 3 times").
  The same rule for each story shelf.

## Owner's decisions (pop-ups, 23 Oct 2026 AEDT)
1. **Schedule:** the full next round (r90), not batches spread over several rounds.
2. **What counts:** finished 3 times: the child reaches the game's celebration 3 times (any level); opening and leaving
   at once does not count; under-5s count any finish. For stories: read or watched to the last page 3 times.
3. **Badges:** a badge per category, earned when the category's next games unlock, shown on the shirt and in the sticker
   book. The badge marks the unlock; it is not an extra condition.
4. **Grown-ups:** a Studio switch on Releases, "Unlock all games and stories" (every device or the ticked ones), off by
   default; testers and auto play can reach everything with it.
