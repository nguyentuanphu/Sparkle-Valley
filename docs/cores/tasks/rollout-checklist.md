# Studio checklist: the batch rollout (owner's decisions, 13 Oct AEDT)

Where: Sparkle Studio, **Ra mắt (Releases)**. For each row: switch **On**, type the **start date**, set **New days** to 7
(leave "on for N days" empty). Dates are Saturdays; a feature starts at midnight on the device (AEDT for the family).

**Do this only after the release with the rollout is live** (core/rollout, merged by Release). Before that the game does
not yet remember who has used what, and a start date would hide things from every child.

What a start date does:
- Before the date: hidden from children who never used the feature. A child who already used it keeps it.
- Tester devices see it 7 days early, by themselves. The ticked "sneak peeks" list still works as well.
- On the date: on for everyone, with the MỚI badge and the "New in the valley!" card (up to 3 at a time) for 7 days.

**No row (leave as they are):** the festivals (Halloween 31 Oct, Christmas 25 Dec, New Year 31 Dec and the rest) come by
their own dates on the Festivals page; a start date on a festival's switch would also hide its build-up weeks. The base
game has no switch to set: the world and its games, the story prologue and chapters 1 and 2, the Simple screen, the shop,
family missions, bedtime and the other grown-up features, traffic, signposts and the bug fixes.

**What a child keeps:** a child who already used a held-back feature keeps it. The game counts what a save already shows
(jobs done, Ông Cú's lessons heard, surprises and festival hunt finds, mystery boxes opened, collection items, Hội làng
prizes, chapter 3 and yard steps, the companion fed or given a hat, food bought, money and reading games played) and from
this release on, every real use. Before this release nothing recorded bicycle, xích lô, vehicle and bus rides,
"Where is...?", villager chats or singers, so a child who used those only before the update waits for its batch day.
