# Sparkle Valley · Thung Lũng Lấp Lánh — Handoff

A bilingual (English / Tiếng Việt) 3D open-world browser game for the owner's children, aged 2–8.
Attach this file at the start of a new chat, with **`docs/SO-TAY-THIET-KE.md` (Sổ tay thiết kế, the design handbook)**, which holds every design, data, safety and release rule in one place, and **`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`**, the authoritative architecture contract: layers, systems, entity model, ids, events, lifecycle, the SYSTEM-NNN hard rules, tests, the module audit and the migration plan. Read both before changing code. Code: **https://github.com/nguyentuanphu/Sparkle-Valley** (work on a `claude/…` branch, merged into `main` by pull request).

## 0. Ground rules (the owner set these — follow them every time)

1. **Do not change, remove or restyle any existing feature or UI unless the owner asks for that change.** Layout, colours, fonts, icons, button order, menu tabs, Grown-ups sections, texts and game rules stay as they are. New work goes *alongside*.
2. **HARD RULE: never `git push`, and never open or update a pull request, until the owner has checked the work and clearly said to push.** Commit locally, report what changed and how it was tested, give the Netlify zip **and the local test bundle** (`tools/make-bundle.sh <folder> --parts`, which also splits it into parts under 28 MB for sending: always the full game with voices + SparkleStudio.exe + HANDOFF.md + `docs/INFRASTRUCTURE.md` + ARCHITECTURE.md + the handbook), then wait. One "yes, push" covers one push. (Also in `CLAUDE.md`.) **Google Drive is a second source of truth (owner, 4 Oct): everything uploaded to GitHub also goes to Google Drive.** The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/drive-mirror.yml` copies every pushed branch to the owner's Drive folder "Sparkle Valley" → "GitHub mirror/<branch>/" (rclone; one-time setup by the owner: secrets `RCLONE_CONF` and `DRIVE_FOLDER_ID`, steps at the top of that file). Each round the read-me in that folder is updated with the round and the bundle parts (Claude's own Drive connection only uploads small text files, so it can't copy the repository itself). **Standing instruction (owner, 4 Oct, after Round E):** "Yes push, all other rounds keep running without asking me": the planned rounds run one after another, each pushed once its full suite is green, each still reported; it lasts until the owner says otherwise.
3. **Run the regression suite before and after every change** (§2). The `contract` check fails if the UI's structure changed; run `--update-contract` only when the owner asked for that UI change, and say so.
4. Every text is bilingual: `T('English', 'Tiếng Việt')`. Spoken lines: `say(en, vi)`. People's titles follow the language (`NAMES_EN` / `NAMES_VI`, `registerName()`); 3D signs take `['English', 'Tiếng Việt']` or a function and redraw on `onLang()`.
5. On every change **bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`** and add new files to `FILES`, or phones keep the old version.
6. **Never put API keys or personal data in the code or the repo.** Keys live only in the grown-up's settings on their device. The ElevenLabs key pasted in an early chat must be replaced after use.
7. **House number 8:** only the number "8" may appear. The street name must never appear in the game or the repo.
8. Test at phone (320–390 px), tablet and desktop sizes, with 1 and 2 players, in both languages, and say plainly what could not be tested.
9. Keep it fun and safe for ages 2–8, girls and boys. Under-5 mode never punishes (no losing, no timers, no reading needed); big kids can have real challenge.
10. **HARD RULES for land and roads (round 29, checked by `flat` and `roadshape`):** every village, town and city is one flat level (`LEVELS` in world.js, applied last in `heightAt`; only the river, the lake, ponds, bridge ramps, road surfaces and a building's own base pad differ). Roads are straight runs joined by true circular curves (fillets: asphalt 24 m, dirt 16 m), run straight into every gate, are never steeper than **8 % (dirt 12 %)** (owner, round 30: `ROAD_GRADE` in roads.js, no allowance; where a road can't meet it, the place is lowered: China Town 4.5 m, India Village 5.5 m, Bắc Ninh 8 m), have every change of slope rounded, take the place's level inside a village and the through road's exact height at a junction, and have a soft verge with flowers along both sides. Never fill the river (bridge banks and road beds stay off it).
11. **HARD: the downloaded (recorded Azure) voices are always the voice, on every device including iPhone.** Every new spoken line must end up in the pack: run `tools/voice-harvest.mjs` and `tools/make-voice-pack.mjs --engine azure` at the end of a round. Checked by `voice` (round 30, after the owner heard the iPhone voice on Safari).

14. **Suggestions and anything not finished: always ask the owner before doing them.** At the end of every round, list the suggestions and the partly-done items and let the owner decide which to do (never start them on your own).
15. **Player numbers and Sparkle Studio online (owner chose B and C in round 30, built):** the anonymous player counter, the Studio Players page and the Studio online at `/studio/` are built. **Sparkle Studio online and SparkleStudio.exe must always be updated together**: they share `studio/ui` and `studio/core.cjs`, `studio/version.json` must equal the game's BUILD (checked by `studio`), and the exe is rebuilt every round `studio/` changes.

## 0b. Design rules (HARD RULES, owner round 30; follow G0–G11 and R1–R12 in every round)

Merged from the owner's "Vietnamese Village Hard Prompts" and the game's measurable rules. Where the code is checked by a test, the check is named; anything new must pass the same checks.

**G0 Style anchor** (start every art or feature brief with it): "Colourful polished Vietnamese village game art, soft warm afternoon lighting, clean toon shading with soft dark-brown outlines (#3a2a20), chunky rounded shapes, harmonious saturated pastels (pink #ff6fae, purple #8a5cf6, leaf green #43c463, gold #ffb020, sky #29b6f6, paper cream #fff6e4), no clutter, readable silhouettes, friendly for children aged 2–8."

**Global**
- **G1 Toon:** `toonVC()`/MeshToonMaterial + `outline()`; rounded shapes; no realistic/PBR textures; no sharp spikes at child height.
- **G2 Scale, 1 unit = 1 m:** child 1.6 m (3 heads), adult 2.0 m, grandparents 1.8 m slightly bent; doors ≥ 1.4 × 2.4 m; steps ≤ 0.4 m.
- **G3 Bilingual everything:** `T('EN','VI')` / `['EN','VI']`, redrawn on `onLang()`; handwritten-style signs too.
- **G4 On the ground:** `heightAt(x,z)` ± 0.05 m; footprints levelled (FLATS / `levelUnderBuildings`).
- **G5 Nothing on a road or its verge:** no props, trees, rocks, grass tufts, flowers, pickups or NPC spots within `road.w/2 + 1.5 m` of a road centre (`roadDist()`). *(checked: `roads`)*
- **G6** Fronts face the nearest road or yard; the interaction point is on the road side.
- **G7 Interactables:** a floating icon, ~2.5 m tap radius, one main action.
- **G8 Safe for ages 2–8:** no weapons, blood, scary faces, animal fights, real logos, phone numbers or addresses; house 8 shows only "8".
- **G9 "High detail" = silhouette and colour, not polygons:** small props ≤ ~2,000 triangles, InstancedMesh for repeats, works at quality 'low'.
- **G10** Existing UI and features unchanged unless asked; bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`; add new files to `FILES`; tests before and after.
- **G11 No clutter:** at most one big focal object per 10 m of street, ≥ 40 % open ground, nothing blocking a walking line.

**Roads**
- **R1** Three classes, constant width end to end: asphalt 5.2 m (villages and cities), dirt 3.4 m (quiet places), footpath 1.6 m (inside villages, round ponds, along paddy bunds).
- **R2 Continuous curvature:** min radius 10 m asphalt, 6 m dirt, 3 m footpath; no S-bends within 15 m, no zigzags. *(The game meets this with true circular arcs, fillets of 24 m asphalt / 16 m dirt, rather than Chaikin smoothing; checked: `roadshape`, no step turns more than 8°.)*
- **R3 Surface:** max `heightAt` of the centre and 4 points 1.4 m away + 0.08 m; never > 0.3 m above the ground (flatten instead); grade ≤ 8 % asphalt, 12 % dirt, 15 % footpath (steps above). *(Enforced exactly by `roadshape` since round 30.)*
- **R4 Clear corridor:** `w/2 + 1.5 m` (0.8 m for footpaths); trees ≥ `w/2 + 2.5 m`; buildings 2–4 m back from the edge.
- **R5 Junctions:** T by default: the side road leaves at 60–120°, stops at the main road's edge, runs straight ≥ 6 m before curving. Y only where a road splits evenly into two equal roads (each arm 30–60° off the old heading). Inner corners filleted (radius ≥ road width); the junction is one plain asphalt patch without lines: no gaps, no overlaps. *(checked: `roads`, every side road that ends on another road's middle leaves it at 60–120°.)*
- **R6** Outside towns only T-junctions; crossroads and roundabouts only inside towns; junctions on one road ≥ 20 m apart; parallel roads ≥ 12 m apart; roads never cross without a junction.
- **R7 Gates:** a named road end coincides with its gate anchor (0 m gap, no grass strip); the surface runs 2 m through the gate into the street or yard with no step; gate perpendicular, posts outside the road edge, opening ≥ road + 1 m each side; bilingual sign, letters ≥ 0.8 m.
- **R8** Every village and city reachable by asphalt; no dead ends except at a gate, landmark or pier; real geography north → south. *(checked: `roads`)*
- **R9 River crossings:** within ± 20° of perpendicular, straight ≥ 6 m each side; asphalt on a flat bridge at road level; dirt roads and footpaths on a low wooden arched bridge (≤ 1.2 m rise, 0.9 m rails); nothing standing on bridge decks.
- **R10** Lines only on asphalt; plain asphalt at junctions; zebra crossings only in towns, perpendicular.
- **R11 Pond footpaths** follow the shore 1.5–2.5 m from the water, never < 1 m; gaps end at a bench, pier or bridge, never across a game board or doorway.
- **R12 Soft edges:** a 0.3–0.5 m colour blend from road to grass; no jagged hard edge.

**By category**
- **Village houses:** curved red tile roof + small thatched porch, warm wood/ochre walls with a small painted flower panel, stone base, potted flowers, short bamboo fence; NO modern elements in villages (cities keep their tube houses, villas, weatherboards, Queenslanders).
- **Kiosks:** fixed 2.4 × 1.6 m, or a wooden push cart 2.2 × 1.2 m on two wheels; counter 0.9 m. Vietnam: red-and-yellow striped canopy, red, yellow and green paper lanterns, ≤ 3 baskets of tropical fruit; handwritten-style bilingual sign, letters ≥ 0.25 m.
- **Market day (chợ phiên):** 3–6 cloth-awning stalls, aisles ≥ 3 m, one product per stall.
- **Vehicles:**
  - On dirt roads and footpaths, only buffalo carts (red canopy, hay, tiny lotus), bicycles with flower and fruit baskets, and cyclos, ≤ 1 m/s.
  - On asphalt, only buses and scooters (riders in helmets), ≤ 6 m/s; keep right in Vietnam, left in Australia.
  - Every vehicle waits for a child within 4 m in front, and never knocks anyone.
- **Animals:** buffalo, hen, dog, cat, duck: happy faces, big eyes, flat toon colours (no fur strands), never bite, fight or get hurt; tap = sound + bilingual name.
- **NPCs:** áo bà ba, farmer clothes, nón lá, warm skin tones, gentle idle poses, bilingual titles, never caricatured.
- **Village landmarks:** banyan with hanging roots and a small shrine at its foot; stone well with a small tiled roof and a bucket nearby ("cây đa, giếng nước, sân đình"); village gate with stone pillars, red lanterns and a bilingual board, on the gate anchor (R7). Respectful near shrines.
- **River:** wooden đò with a rower in nón lá, thuyền thúng, lotus near the banks (never in ferry lanes), piers ≥ 6 m with rails and a life ring; children never drown.
- **Map:** north up, compass rose (red N), legend panel, thin bamboo-and-lotus border ≤ 8 % width, bilingual title, labels ≥ 12 px.
- **UI:** the current UI stays the default. A wooden lotus-carved "Làng xưa" skin is OPTIONAL only (Phase 5, only if the owner says yes), contrast ≥ 4.5:1, primary buttons ≥ 56 px.

**How the owner wants this work done (the design-rules plan, one phase per round):**
1. **Phase 1** roads (junction angles, grass off roads, faster `roadDist`, checks).
2. **Phase 2** Lotus Village (well, shrine, pond footpath, thuyền thúng, real hens, dog and cat, bicycles, buffalo cart). **Done in round 30 (round B), `js/lotus.js`.**
3. **Phase 3** kiosks, push carts, market day, NPC clothing.
4. **Phase 4** map decoration.
5. **Phase 5** the optional "Làng xưa" skin.

In each phase: run the suite before and after, take before/after screenshots from the same spots, commit locally, give the Netlify zip, and wait for "yes, push".

## 1. Run, deploy, install

- **Run:** `python3 -m http.server 8765` in the repo → http://localhost:8765. `window.__sv` is the game object (heroes, world, farm, partners, playGame…).
- **Netlify (main site):** `./build-netlify.sh` zips `index.html sw.js manifest.webmanifest netlify.toml css js fonts icons vendor videos content` (+ `voices/` if present). Drag the zip onto the **same** Netlify site's Deploys page. Saves belong to the web address, so a new "Netlify Drop" site starts empty.
- **GitHub Pages** auto-deploys `main` (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`). It needs a one-time setup: repo → Settings → Pages → Source: **GitHub Actions** (a run failed with "Get Pages site failed … Not Found" until this was done). For a private repo on a free plan, Pages is not available; use Netlify only.
- **Sparkle Studio (grown-ups' dashboard, Windows):** `studio/studio.cjs` is a small local web server (127.0.0.1:7777, no dependencies) with the dashboard in `studio/ui/`. `bash studio/build-exe.sh` packs both into `studio/dist/SparkleStudio.exe` (Node single executable, ~86 MB, marked a Windows GUI app so no console window; not in git: `studio/dist/`, `studio/.build/` are ignored). Put the exe in the game folder (or choose the folder in its Settings) and double-click: the dashboard opens in its own Edge/Chrome app window; ⏻ Close stops it, and it stops by itself 3 minutes after its window is closed. Messages go to `%APPDATA%\SparkleStudio\studio.log`. Pages: Home, Message, Villagers (names, photo faces, voice woman/man), Stories, Family quiz, **Map & building** (modes: villagers; build houses and buildings from `BUILD_TYPES` in `js/worldedit.js`, houses become visitable homes with a name and owner; move or remove anything inside a circle; flat land rectangles or grow a whole place; "show the buildings that are there now" opens the game out of sight and reads its buildings), **Real photos** (the best photos of every landmark from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons search and Openverse, a few from each, into `photos/` with credits and licences), **Ads** (off; pictures, words, link, where, how many a day, and a guide), **Defaults** (rest timer, language, bilingual, money, captions, calm, text size, music: applied once on each device whenever they change), Children (open/edit a `.txt` backup), Voices (count missing lines incl. `tools/voice-extra.json`; make them with Azure or Piper or the microphone; import a device's backup to add the lines it said with the machine voice), **Website** (Netlify deploys with which is live, rebuild, make an older deploy live again; GitHub latest commits and open pull requests with Merge), Publish (**to GitHub in two steps, like online (round 30)**: **1. Save a draft** sends only the changed files of `content/ voices/ photos/ tools/voice-extra.json` to the `studio-draft` branch; **2. Publish** puts the draft on `main` as one commit and Netlify rebuilds; or the zip / Netlify upload), Check, Settings. Keys (Azure, Netlify, GitHub fine-grained token) live only in `%APPDATA%\SparkleStudio\settings.json`. Without the exe: `node studio/studio.cjs` (Node 22). Its backups of `content/custom.json` go to `content/old/` (ignored, never published).
- **Infrastructure (hosting, functions, keys by name, services, local testing, release checklist):** `docs/INFRASTRUCTURE.md`. Keep it current.
- **How the live site updates:** Netlify site `sparklevalley` is linked to this GitHub repo and publishes `main` (PRs get deploy previews). So: merge a PR → live in a minute or two; Sparkle Studio's "Publish to GitHub" commits to `main` → live the same way. Devices pick up new files the next time the game opens online (service worker; content, voices and photos are kept for offline).
- **Family content pack:** `content/custom.json` (+ `content/faces/*.jpg`) is read at start by `js/custom.js`: daily message, names, faces, `genders`, own tales, family quiz, `npcs` (villagers placed on the map, spawned in `main.js`), `world` (`build`, `edits`, `land`: js/worldedit.js adds buildings before the homes are made and moves/removes after everything is built; `land` becomes FLATS in world.js), `defaults` (applied once per change, signature in `settings.packDefaults`). A device's own renames and settings always win. Written by the Studio; `{}` means nothing extra.
- **Phones:** close and reopen the app to pick up a new version. iPhone: play from the Home Screen icon (Safari may clear data of sites not opened for weeks). Backups to iCloud/Drive move progress between devices.

**Why a line can still use the machine voice, and how it is fixed:** the pack only has sentences known before playing. Lines built while playing (names, numbers, colours, items: 171 of the 302 places that speak) are collected by `node tools/voice-harvest.mjs` (plays every game and taps everything in both languages, writes `tools/voice-extra.json`), and by each device: a line said without a recording is noted (`sv2_voicemiss`, shown in Grown-ups → Voices, carried in the backup file, imported in Studio → Voices). `make-voice-pack.mjs` and the Studio include `voice-extra.json`. When a whole line has no recording, its recorded sentences still play from the pack and only the rest uses the device voice (`speak` in `js/voice.js`). Women, girls and grandmas use the woman's recording, men, boys and grandpas the man's (`speakerKind`: titles in both languages, girls' names, Studio genders). ## 7. Decisions and known limits

- Natural voices, voice cloning and AI chat are optional, online and use the parent's own keys; everything works offline without them.
- Online play uses the public PeerJS server to introduce devices; voice goes directly; game messages fall back to the cloud relay. The relay key comes from the 4-digit room code, so names and positions are only lightly protected from someone who knows the scheme; photos have their own pair lock (ECDH). Anyone who joins a room is a player and appears in the room list; the room's starter can remove them (works on both routes, and they can't come back into that room). Only the starter can say "full" or remove someone; ids, looks, friend codes and positions from others are checked before use (they go into the page). Topic names use a slow hash so friend codes can't be read back from them. Newcomers knock and the starter lets them in (saved friends straight in); the owner kept 4-digit codes. The QR code was removed (owner, round D), and with it the idea of a per-room secret in it: the relay key stays the room code. Rate and size limits per message kind are in `js/netmsg.js`.
- Graphics follow the setting (Fast / Auto / Pretty); Auto picks Fast on phones and then raises the sharpness step by step while the phone keeps up (adaptive resolution, `adaptResolution` in `main.js`).
- iOS Home Screen apps keep storage separate from Safari: use backups to move progress.
- Stamina is counted in wheels (1–3); food and juice give one extra wheel.
- Saves: if the main save is missing and its copy can't be read within 8 s, this session never overwrites the copies (new progress goes to `new-…`), and the restore also looks at the daily copies (the last 10 are kept).
- **Level land grows by itself:** every building, house, kiosk and landmark gets a level footprint (`FOOTPRINTS`, filled by `levelUnderBuildings` after the world is built), and `widenFlatLand` widens every flat (`WIDE` = 10 m) at the very end, moving the ground, trees, roads and props up or down with it so nothing shifts on the map. World building places things with `oldHeightAt` (the heights before widening), so adding a new building only needs it placed: its ground levels itself.
- Inside the cloud sandbox, Node's `fetch` needs `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt` for the voice-pack tool (otherwise Azure answers 403).
- **Roads and the river:** roads.js `profile()` works out each road's height (fixed parts: bridge decks, village levels, gate ends, junction overlaps; long average; grade limit; rounding) and registers road beds; `heightAt` shapes the ground under and beside each road to it (wider banks for deeper cuttings), so walking on a road is walking on the ground (`world.roadTop` is null). `world.roadLines` keeps every road's points and heights (tests, verges, flowers). The river line is `RIVER` in world.js (ferry.js uses the same).
- **Village games time (js/folkshow.js):** the folk-game hosts of each village take turns on the real clock (150 s slots, 75 s of play), a board counts down, 3 coins start the next game now; a few children dance round the host; one singer per village (bird stage names, renameable) sings traditional songs on the synthesiser with the words in a bubble.
- **Boats:** the lake ferry and the river raft line (up and down the river past the towns, under both bridges) ask "Where to?" and go straight there; the two crossing rafts go to the other bank. While on a boat the camera stays under bridge decks.
- **Friends meet by themselves:** `G.autoMeet` (main.js): with friends on the list and sharing on (switched on by adding a friend), the friend whose code comes first opens a room and the other joins it from their status. Grown-ups → Online play can switch it off.
- **Ads (js/ads.js):** nothing unless `content/custom.json` has `ads.enabled: true` (Sparkle Studio → Ads, with a guide). Signboards near towns and a small card after a mini-game, labelled, no tracking, links behind a grown-up sum. **Hip roofs:** `P()` in gfx.js turns a 4-sided cone before stretching it, so a long building's roof stays square on its walls.
- Leafy spots, shrines and towers are fixed in `world.js`; heritage sites, crops and culture groups find a clear spot (off roads) near their village by themselves.
