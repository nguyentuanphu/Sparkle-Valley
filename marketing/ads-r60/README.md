# Sparkle Valley · five ads for r60 (English and Tiếng Việt)

Ten vertical videos (1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 30 to 40 s): five ads, each made once fully in English and once
fully in Vietnamese (voice and captions). Made 11 Oct 2026 (Sydney time) from real screenshots of the live game (r60).
The voice is Azure neural speech (English: en-AU Natasha; Vietnamese: vi-VN Hoài My); the music is the ad kit's own
generated jingle (no samples, no licence issues). For TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Stories.

**Every video has a thumbnail** (owner, 11 Oct): `<video>-thumb.jpg` next to it (1080 × 1920, the ad's headline, its key picture, the logo and sparklevalley.au), also built into each mp4 as its cover picture. Upload it as the cover on TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook.

**The website is sparklevalley.au** (owner, 11 Oct): every end card and caption sends people there.

## Key selling points (all true of r60, live 11 Oct)

1. **A gentle 3D world for ages 2 to 8.** Under-fives never lose and never race a clock; every try is cheered.
2. **Learning hidden in play:** 77 mini-games (counting, colours, letters, memory, thinking, folk games, music).
3. **English ⇄ Tiếng Việt** for everything, spoken aloud; Vietnamese words said by the Vietnamese voice.
4. **Vietnamese heritage:** tales such as Thánh Gióng told with moving pictures; Trung Thu and other festivals in their
   real season; Hà Nội, Hội An, Lotus Village.
5. **Two homes, one valley:** Vietnam and Australia (Sydney Harbour, ferries), the lantern story with a little dragon friend.
6. **Grown-ups stay in charge:** a rest timer, a gentle bedtime, family missions written by the parent.
7. **Safe by design:** online play is off until a grown-up turns it on; friends join with a six-digit code and the
   microphone stays off until they are let in.
8. **Play together:** two children on one tablet (side by side or face to face), or family far away online.
9. **Works offline** once opened; runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer (Add to Home Screen).

Not claimed on purpose: "no ads" (the game has an ads feature, switched off) and prices.

## Re-making the ads

Videos in `marketing/ads-r60/`. Source in `marketing/source/`: `ads5.js` (the five ads: scenes, captions and voice lines in both languages),
`voice5.mjs` (records the voice lines with Azure; needs `AZURE_SPEECH_KEY` and `AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`),
`render5.mjs` (draws the frames on the stage), `make5.sh` (frames, music, voice mix, mp4), `stills5/` (the r60 stills,
`<name>-<lang>.jpg`). The stills come from `capture5.mjs <lang> stills5 [cine|screens]` (game served on SV_URL; `CINE` = a JSON list of camera views, `ONLY` = some screens).

```bash
cd marketing/source
node voice5.mjs                       # only lines whose mp3 is missing are recorded
python3 -m http.server 8770 &         # serves the stage
./make5.sh                            # all five, both languages -> out5/sparkle-valley-<ad>-<lang>.mp4
node render5.mjs <ad> <lang> thumb    # the thumbnail -> out5/sparkle-valley-<ad>-<lang>-thumb.jpg
./make5.sh parents                    # just one ad (LANGS=en ./make5.sh for English only)
```
