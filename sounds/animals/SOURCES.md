# Animal sounds: where they come from

Real recordings of the ten animals in "Who Says That?" (owner, 17 Oct 2026 AEDT: "need real voice for animals"),
also heard from the animals in the valley and the sticker book. Each was cut to its strongest call (1 to 2.7 s),
faded in and out, made even in loudness (loudnorm -16 LUFS) and saved as 48 kbps mono MP3.
